FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our foundation.

What is Juice World Foundation Uganda?

We are a community-based foundation dedicated to supporting vulnerable children through food assistance, education support, community care, and hope.

How can I donate?

You can support us through MTN Mobile Money or by contacting us on WhatsApp for other donation options.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. We welcome volunteers who want to make a positive difference in children’s lives.

Can organizations partner with you?

Yes. We welcome NGOs, businesses, churches, schools, and community groups that want to work with us.

Where are you located?

Wakiso, Kampala, Uganda.
