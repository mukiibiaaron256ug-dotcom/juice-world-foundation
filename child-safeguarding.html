Child Safeguarding Policy

Protecting every child with dignity, respect, and care.

Our Commitment

Juice World Foundation Uganda is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and supportive environment for every child. The wellbeing of children is our highest priority.

Our Principles

  • Every child deserves protection from abuse, neglect, exploitation, and discrimination.
  • Every child will be treated with dignity and respect.
  • The safety of children comes before all other interests.
  • Concerns about a child's safety will be taken seriously and addressed appropriately.

Our Expectations

All staff members, volunteers, partners, and visitors are expected to act responsibly, behave respectfully, and help create a safe environment for children.

Reporting Concerns

If anyone has concerns about the safety or wellbeing of a child connected with our foundation, they should contact Juice World Foundation Uganda immediately so appropriate action can be taken.

Our Promise

We will continue improving our safeguarding practices to ensure that every child who interacts with our foundation is protected, respected, and valued.
