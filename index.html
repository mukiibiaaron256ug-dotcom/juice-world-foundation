About Us

Juice World Foundation is a community initiative in Uganda dedicated to helping vulnerable children by providing food, care, and hope for a better future.

Our Story

We started Juice World Foundation because we believe that no child should go hungry. Although we have limited resources, we currently support around 30 children and dream of expanding our work with the help of kind supporters.

Our Mission

To help the new innocent generation by providing food, care, hope, education, and opportunities for a brighter future.
