About Us

Juice World Foundation Uganda is a community-based non-profit organization dedicated to improving the lives of vulnerable children through food support, education, healthcare and community development.

Our Story

Juice World Foundation Uganda was created from a desire to give hope to children facing poverty and hardship. We currently support around 30 children and continue working to expand our impact with the support of donors, volunteers and partners.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children through education, food assistance, healthcare and community empowerment.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every child grows up healthy, educated, safe and full of hope.

Our Programs

Education

Helping vulnerable children access education through school fees, books, uniforms and learning materials.

Food Support

Providing nutritious meals and emergency food assistance to children and families in need.

Healthcare

Supporting children with access to medical care, health education and essential treatment.

Community Empowerment

Working with families and communities to build sustainable solutions that improve children's lives.
