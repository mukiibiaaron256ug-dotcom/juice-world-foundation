Welcome to Juice World Foundation Uganda

Juice World Foundation Uganda is a Christian, God-fearing community organization committed to bringing hope, love, and practical support to vulnerable children and families throughout Uganda. We believe every child deserves food, education, protection, encouragement, and the opportunity to achieve their dreams.

About Us

Juice World Foundation Uganda was founded with a vision of helping vulnerable children regardless of their background. Through God's love and the support of our partners, volunteers, and well-wishers, we continue to bring hope to children who need it most.

❤️ Love

We serve every child with compassion and respect.

🙏 Faith

Our work is guided by Christian values and integrity.

🌍 Community

We believe strong communities build brighter futures.

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children through food support, education, healthcare, spiritual encouragement, community outreach, and sustainable development.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every child grows with hope, love, education, protection, and equal opportunities to succeed.

Our Main Projects

🍛 Feeding Program

Providing meals and nutritional support for vulnerable children.

📚 Education Support

Helping children remain in school through educational support.

❤️ Community Care

Supporting families through outreach and community development.

🌱 Future Projects

Healthcare, shelter, vocational training, and child empowerment.
