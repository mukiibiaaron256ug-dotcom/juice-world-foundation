Welcome, You Are Part of Our Family

Juice World Foundation Uganda is a Christian, God-fearing community organisation committed to bringing hope, love and practical support to vulnerable African children and families throughout Uganda. Every child in our care is welcomed, valued and given the chance to learn, grow and dream of a brighter future.
