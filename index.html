Welcome

A community built on faith, food and family.

Juice World Foundation Uganda is a Christian, God-fearing community organisation committed to bringing hope, love and practical support to vulnerable children and families throughout Uganda. Every meal shared and every child encouraged is a step toward a brighter future.
