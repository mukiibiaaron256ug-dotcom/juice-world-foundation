Juice World Foundation

📖 Our Story

Juice World Foundation Uganda was founded with a vision of bringing hope to vulnerable children and families through God's love. Our journey began with a desire to help those in need by providing food, care, encouragement and opportunities for a brighter future.

🙏 Our Christian Foundation

We are a Christian, God-fearing organization. We believe that every child is created by God with value, purpose and potential. Our work is guided by faith, compassion, honesty and service.

❤️ What We Do

  • Provide food support.
  • Support children's education.
  • Community outreach.
  • Spiritual encouragement.
  • Support vulnerable families.

🌍 Our Dream

Our dream is to see every child grow in a safe environment with access to education, food, love and hope, becoming responsible citizens who transform their communities.
