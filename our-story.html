Our Story

Every child deserves hope, love, and a brighter future.

How We Started

Juice World Foundation Uganda was founded with one simple belief: no child should go hungry or lose hope because of poverty.

We began by helping vulnerable children in our community with the little resources we had. Although our journey started small, our vision has always been big—to build a future where every child has food, education, care, and the opportunity to succeed.

Who We Help

Today we support vulnerable children by providing food assistance, encouragement, and community support. We continue to dream of expanding our services to include education, healthcare, shelter, and life skills for every child in need.

Our Vision

To build a future where every child grows up healthy, educated, safe, and filled with hope.

Our Promise

Every donation, partnership, and act of kindness helps transform lives. Together, we can give children the opportunity to dream, learn, and build a better tomorrow.
