Our Story

A journey that began with a desire to help.

Juice World Foundation Uganda was founded with a vision of bringing hope to vulnerable children and families through God’s love.

How it began

Food, care and opportunity for a brighter future.

Our journey began with a desire to help those in need by providing food, care, encouragement and opportunities for a brighter future. What started as a simple act of compassion has grown into a foundation dedicated to serving Uganda’s next generation.

🙏 Our Christian foundation

Guided by faith, compassion, honesty and service.

We are a Christian, God-fearing organization. We believe that every child is created by God with value, purpose and potential. Our work is guided by faith, compassion, honesty and service.

❤️ What we do

Four commitments to every child we serve.

🍜

Food Support

Providing meals and nutrition to vulnerable children.

📚

Education

Supporting children’s education whenever possible.

🤝

Community Outreach

Reaching families throughout the community.

🙏

Spiritual Encouragement

Walking alongside families in faith and hope.

🌍 Our dream

Every child growing in a safe environment — with education, food, love and hope.

Our dream is to see every child grow in a safe environment with access to education, food, love and hope, becoming responsible citizens who transform their communities.
