Founder

My name is MUKIIBI AARON, and I am the developer of Juice World Foundation Uganda. I am passionate about serving vulnerable children and families by promoting hope, love, compassion, and positive change in our communities. I am committed to supporting the foundation's mission of caring for those in need, encouraging unity, and inspiring others to make a difference. I believe that together we can build a brighter future for every child and family we serve. "Giving Hope, Love, and a Better Future to Every Child.".
