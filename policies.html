Our Policies

Serving with integrity, compassion, faith, and respect.

Our Foundation

Juice World Foundation Uganda is built on Christian values. We believe in serving God by showing love, compassion, honesty, and kindness to people in need, especially vulnerable children and families.

Our Mission

We are committed to helping vulnerable children and families through practical support, encouragement, and community development while treating every person with dignity and respect.

Respect for Everyone

While our foundation is inspired by Christian faith, we welcome and serve people from all backgrounds without discrimination. We believe every person deserves care, love, and respect.

Child Protection

We are committed to protecting every child from abuse, neglect, exploitation, and any form of harm. The safety and wellbeing of children are always our highest priority.

Integrity and Accountability

We are committed to using donations responsibly, acting honestly, and maintaining transparency in our work and relationships with donors, partners, volunteers, and the communities we serve.

Our Core Values

  • Faith in God
  • Love and Compassion
  • Honesty and Integrity
  • Respect for Every Person
  • Service to the Community
  • Teamwork and Unity
  • Hope for Every Child
